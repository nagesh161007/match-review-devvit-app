### Third-party sports data (SportMonks)

We use SportMonks (api.sportmonks.com) to retrieve public football match 
information such as fixtures, scores, lineups, and player details. This data 
powers the match review and player rating experience.

Requests to SportMonks are made from our server-side Devvit app using an API 
key. We do not share Reddit account information or user voting data with 
SportMonks.
